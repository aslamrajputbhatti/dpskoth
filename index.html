About Diamond Public School

Diamond Public School Koth Kalan, Hisar is a well-known general education school serving families in the Koth Kalan area of Haryana. It combines academic discipline with a caring environment that helps students grow in confidence, values, and learning.

Our Legacy

Built on the belief that quality education should nurture both academic excellence and strong character in every child.

Our Vision

To create a vibrant learning environment where students grow into confident, responsible, and capable citizens.

Our Mission

To provide meaningful education through dedicated mentoring, disciplined learning, and holistic development.

Our Values

Respect, integrity, discipline, curiosity, and empathy guide every interaction within the school community.
